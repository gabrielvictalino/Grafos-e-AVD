# Sprites do jogo

Dez imagens geradas individualmente com a ferramenta de geração de imagens, em fundo transparente. Exportadas para PNG RGBA 32 bits, 128 × 128 pixels. O jogo gira o táxi durante o desenho.

Prompt comum: Single isolated game sprite; consistent charming 1990s 32-bit pixel art, crisp pixel edges, warm palette, readable silhouette, square canvas, centered with transparent margins; no labels, no UI, no watermark.

- `carro.png`: one yellow taxi car viewed strictly from directly overhead, front pointing UP, visible dark windshield and small taxi roof sign, no words, no perspective.
- `passageiro_a.png`: one woman with dark skin and curly dark hair, wearing coral red jacket and blue jeans, standing, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.
- `passageiro_b.png`: one elderly man with light skin and white hair, wearing blue jacket and carrying a small bag, standing, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.
- `passageiro_c.png`: one young man with medium brown skin wearing green hoodie and backpack, standing, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.
- `passageiro_d.png`: one woman with light skin and purple hair, wearing purple dress and holding handbag, standing, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.
- `casa.png`: one small cozy house with terracotta tiled roof, cream walls, teal front door and tiny garden, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.
- `loja.png`: one small corner bakery storefront with coral striped awning, cream walls, window and bread display, no text, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.
- `predio.png`: one compact blue-grey apartment building with flat roof and teal windows, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.
- `mercado.png`: one small green grocery shop with green striped awning and produce crates, no text, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.
- `casa_motorista.png`: one small mint green home with dark blue tiled roof and warm yellow front door, tiny flowers, top-down RPG three-quarter overhead sprite.


Os dois sprites adicionais de penalidades foram gerados juntos e exportados separadamente como PNGs transparentes de 128 × 128 pixels.

- `obras.png` — dois cones laranja e brancos.
- `alagamento.png` — poça com ondulações.
